Term — the jargon system

Jargon always explained in place. Every abbreviation the terminal uses (GEX, VEX, OTM%, Sig…) is a <Term> anchored to the TERMS dictionary — a dotted underline that reveals a one-line definition card on hover or keyboard focus. DataTable headers opt in via the column's help key.
