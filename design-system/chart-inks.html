Vol down

This page used to show a “slayer signature” two-tone here — holo-silver up, luminous violet down — as the active default, with three alternates beside it. That is the defect, not the standard. A reader learns the direction language once: while the candles drew a down bar violet, the cumulative delta, the put wall and the exposure grid ON THE SAME SCREEN drew down red, so one panel was speaking a dialect. candleTheme.ts now derives every value from BULL and BEAR — including the volume tints, so no second literal can drift — and there are no alternates. src/lib/palette.test.ts fails if the candles ever stop being the direction tokens.

Gold liquidity LUT (liquidityField.ts)

openthinmoderatedeep shelf
256-entry RGB lookup, gamma-lifted (x^0.72) so mid shelves read. Stops: rgb(16,12,6) → rgb(50,34,10) → rgb(110,74,16) → rgb(176,122,28) → rgb(218,164,46) → rgb(242,196,92) → rgb(255,226,150). Liquidity owns gold — no other surface uses this family. Depth read-out tints: #F0C45C deep · #C89B3C moderate · #7D7D7D thin.
